Forecast of the Relative Oceanic Niño Index

RONI is NOAA's official El Niño index: how much warmer the central Pacific is than the tropics as a whole, in °C, averaged over three months. Above +0.5 is El Niño, below −0.5 is La Niña. The shaded band is the 80% range, sized from how wrong the back-tested forecasts were at each lead.

How good is it? Back-tested skill

Correlation between forecast and what happened, for forecasts made using only data available at the time. 1 is perfect; below about 0.5 is barely useful.

The spring barrier

Same skill, split by the month the forecast was made. Forecasts that have to cross March–May lose skill fastest: El Niño is hardest to call in spring.

Replays: what would it have said back then?

The whole pipeline re-run using only data up to that month (back-test, training and forecast), then laid over what really happened. Unlike the track record, these are made after the fact, so treat them as a test, not proof.
